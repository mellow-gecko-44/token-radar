How the radar scores

implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth factor × maturity factor

The multiple

Each category's median market-cap / TVL among the snapshot's own live-token protocols, shrunk toward the overall median so a category with two members does not set its own multiple, clamped to 0.05–1.5. A lending token earns a lending multiple, a DEX token a DEX multiple — never one hardcoded number for every protocol.

Growth

7-day TVL change, clamped to −50%…+150%, weighted 0.8. A +4,448% week cannot carry a protocol on its own; a −73% week cannot sink one.

Maturity

0.6 + 0.4 × (1 − e−age / 365). A protocol that has run a year with no token scores near its full value; one that launched last week scores less; the curve saturates because a launch that has not happened in five years is worth less waiting on.

What counts as pre-token

A protocol records no token at all: no market cap, no CoinGecko id, no symbol. Wrapped, staked and bridged products of tokens that already trade are excluded, and so are exchanges and versioned pools — a V2 pool is not a new launch. Live-token protocols are kept apart, shown with the market's own values.
